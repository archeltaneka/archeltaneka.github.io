Projects chevron_right Churn Prediction

Customer Churn Prediction Engine

An end-to-end machine learning solution designed to identify high-risk customers and recommend retention strategies, deploying an XGBoost model with 92% accuracy that processes over 50k customer records daily.

psychology_alt The Problem

The client, a mid-sized SaaS telecom company, was experiencing an alarming 12% monthly churn rate, significantly above the industry average. They lacked visibility into which users were likely to leave and, more importantly, why they were leaving.

Marketing efforts were generic and reactive. The goal was to build a predictive engine to flag at-risk accounts 30 days in advance, allowing the customer success team to intervene proactively.

Why XGBoost over Random Forest?

While Random Forest provided a solid baseline accuracy (86%), XGBoost offered superior performance on the imbalanced dataset due to its gradient boosting framework. It also provided better interpretability via SHAP values, which was critical for explaining churn reasons to stakeholders.

2

Handling Class Imbalance with SMOTE

The dataset was heavily skewed (only 15% churn). Standard training led to poor recall. I implemented SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) during the training phase, which improved recall from 45% to 90.5%, ensuring we caught the majority of at-risk customers.
